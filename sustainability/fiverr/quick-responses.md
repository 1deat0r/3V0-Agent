# Quick Responses — pre-order templates

*Save these in Fiverr's Quick Responses. Auto-reply acknowledges instantly
(doesn't count toward response rate), then a manual reply from these fires
within the hour during NZ waking hours. Personalize the bracketed parts —
templated replies are a conversion killer; these are skeletons, not spam.*

---

## QR1 — Generic first contact (any gig)

> Hi [Name] — thanks for reaching out. Happy to help with this. To give you an
> accurate quote and delivery time, could you send:
> 1. [gig-specific ask — see below]
> 2. Anything that's already been tried
> I'll come back within a couple of hours with a straight answer on whether
> this fits my packages.

## QR2 — Debug gig: what I need

> For a bug fix I need three things: the script or repo, the exact error or
> unexpected output (screenshot is fine), and what you *expected* to happen.
> With those I can usually confirm the tier the same day — I reproduce the
> failure before I quote the fix.

## QR3 — Debug gig: won't reproduce (honesty policy)

> Straight answer: I couldn't reproduce this on my side, and I won't patch
> blind. Here's exactly what I ruled out and the one thing I'd need to
> reproduce it ([specific]). If you'd like, the diagnosis tier covers this —
> or no charge if we stop here.

## QR4 — Code review: scope check

> For a review I need the code (file or repo link), roughly how many lines,
> and what "correct" looks like — e.g. "it processes orders; I'm worried
> about edge cases." I'll confirm the tier by line count: up to 500 lines
> (Basic), 2,000 (Standard), full audit + remediation plan (Premium).

## QR5 — Automation: scope check

> Describe the task as input → output: what you have, what you want, one
> example of each. I'll confirm whether it fits a Basic script or needs the
> Standard/Premium pipeline, and whether it's honestly better as a no-code
> workflow (I'll say so if it is).

## QR6 — AI agent: the three killer questions (pre-empt them)

> Good questions to settle now, and my answers: **What does it cost to run?**
> You bring your own API key (OpenAI/Anthropic/DeepSeek/local), so token
> costs are yours and there are no surprise bills from me. **Who's
> accountable when it errors?** The runbook I deliver covers failure modes
> and recovery steps, and I support the first 30 days. **What's the handoff?**
> Code + config template + runbook + a 30-minute walkthrough call.

## QR7 — Scraping: data contract + ToS honesty

> Before I quote: the target site/pages, the exact fields you want, and how
> often you need it. I'll send a one-page field spec for approval before
> building, and I work within a site's terms — if it blocks or forbids
> scraping, I'll recommend its licensed API instead. Every delivery includes
> a validation report showing exactly what came back empty.

## QR8 — Follow-up after delivery (review request, Fiverr flow only)

> Thanks for the order, [Name] — delivered. Two things that may help: the
> [README / runbook / test command] lets you verify it yourself, and the fix
> carries the 14-day warranty — anything that regresses in that window is on
> me. If it's working well, an honest review helps other buyers find me.
> Anything at all, I'm here.

*Rule: never incentivize a review (bannable). "An honest review helps other
buyers" is the sanctioned phrasing.*
