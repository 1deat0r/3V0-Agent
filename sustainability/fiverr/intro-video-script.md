# Seller intro video — 60-second script

*Fiverr's intro video is a ranking/trust lever we haven't built yet
(COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS §8.8). Shoot on a phone, good light, landscape, one take.
Talk to the camera like a colleague. Optional: screen-share b-roll of the
artifacts while each is mentioned.*

---

**[0:00–0:08] Who and where**
"Hi, I'm [Display Name]. I fix, review, and automate Python code — and I do it
from Wellington, New Zealand, on New Zealand time."

**[0:08–0:25] The promise, concretely**
"Here's how I work, and it's different: I reproduce your bug before I touch
anything — if I can't reproduce it, I'll tell you honestly. Every review I
deliver has a before-and-after fix for *every* finding, not a list of vague
suggestions. Every script ships tested and documented, with a 14-day warranty
on fixes."

**[0:25–0:40] Show the proof**
*(cut to screen b-roll)* "This is a real root-cause report from my portfolio —
reproduction, the exact line, the fix, the test that proves it. And this is
what a scraping order gets: the data, *plus* a validation report that tells
you exactly which fields came back empty. No silent wrong answers."

**[0:40–0:52] AI honesty (turn the stigma into a moat)**
"Full disclosure: I use AI to work faster — and then I hand-verify every line
against your actual code. You're paying for the judgment, not a generated
guess."

**[0:52–1:00] Close**
"Check the portfolio samples in my gigs — they all actually run. Message me
with what's broken, and I'll tell you straight whether I can help."

---

**Shooting notes:** plain background, no AI avatar (buyers in 2026 filter
AI-sounding sellers — a real face is the counter-signal), phone on a tripod,
captions on, under 60s, export 1080p.
